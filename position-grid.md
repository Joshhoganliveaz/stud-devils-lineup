# Stud Devils Position Grid

## Game Format
- 6 innings, coach pitch, 13 players
- 10 fielders per inning (6 infield + 4 outfield), 3 bench
- 8 players play 5 innings (bench once), 5 players play 4 innings (bench twice)

## Rules Applied
- P + 1B combined: max 2 innings per player (forfeiture penalty)
- Max 2 consecutive innings in infield OR outfield (bench resets count)
- No consecutive bench innings
- Jacob never plays 1B
- Julian plays 2B when in the infield
